Recommendation for marketing: a single QR for both stores. If the material will be printed (flyers, posters, cards), it's better for the QR to point to a page of your own, for example https://www.atlasglobalhealth.com/app. That page sends each person to the right place:
- iPhone → https://apps.apple.com/app/id6780929855
- Android → https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bisaseguros.salud.internacional.android
- Computer → a page with both buttons

This has two advantages:
1. One QR works for everyone. With two QRs, people often scan the wrong one.
2. If something changes, you edit the page, not the printed material. For example, if the app moves to another account or you want to measure scans.

Before printing: wait for the app to be published (in both stores, if they use the combined QR) and test the QR on a real iPhone and Android phone. If the material goes out before release, people who scan it will see "not available".
